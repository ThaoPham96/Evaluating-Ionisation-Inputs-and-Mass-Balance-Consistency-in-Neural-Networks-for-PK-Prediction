Proposed result narrative

A pH 7.4 category improves Vd and clearance prediction relative to structure
alone across all three backbones. The category alone carries much of the Vd
signal. Additional pH encodings redistribute compound-level predictions,
with limited evidence of improved held-out error beyond repeated inputs.

Class-median caveat

The class cohort overall-to-class median reduces Vd MAE from 0.5762 to
0.4342 (-24.65%, exploratory 95% interval [-34.10,-16.74]). Numerical
overall-to-class median is 0.5538 to 0.4531 (-18.18% [-28.29,-9.10]).
These medians are estimated in training folds. In the class cohort,
C2 MAEs are 0.4048, 0.4017 and 0.4559. All three C2-class_median pooled
Vd intervals span zero (relative points -6.77%, -7.48%, +5.01%). The
numerical P1 MAEs are 0.4348, 0.4514 and 0.4763. Numerical P1-class_median
has no exported paired CI, so use these point values descriptively or show
the scored numerical P4-class_median contrast in SI. Its three intervals
span zero. Avoid claiming the neural category input beats a class-only
reference generally. The evidence also does not show that the entire
C2-C0 benefit is explained by the median. Within the anionic stratum,
C2-C0 Vd MAE drops by 0.100, 0.083 and 0.058 log10, with the three
exploratory intervals below zero. Chemprop neutral and ChemBERTa cationic
rows also favour C2. Those improvements can reflect within-class shifts
in calibration. Anionic Spearman actually declines in all three model
point estimates (GraphConv 0.449 to 0.384, Chemprop 0.419 to 0.382,
ChemBERTa 0.396 to 0.383), although pooled Vd Spearman increases for all
three. C2 does not resolve lower anionic Vd MAE than class median for any
backbone, and ChemBERTa C2 has higher anionic MAE (0.511 vs 0.390).
Describe the result as substantial class-level signal and some within-class
error shifts, with limited evidence for finer ranking or superiority to
class medians. Within-stratum intervals are exploratory, not a direct test
of differences in effects between classes.

Figure caption conventions

All models predict the same seven PK endpoints jointly, with explicit
supervised weights one and mass-balance penalty zero. Each seed trajectory
pools five held-out parent/scaffold-group outer folds. MAE changes are
arm minus comparator, with negative values favouring the first arm. Five
endpoints use log10 MAE, F/PPB use percentage-point MAE, and the relative
interval bounds use the same fixed comparator MAE as the point estimate.
Prediction shifts instead use signed log2 fold ratios or percentage points.
All prediction changes come from separately fitted models on supplied
representations. The labels contain no added-pH measurements.
