Observed versus predicted Vd and clearance with and without ionisation input.
Page 1: Vd, 259 labelled structures. Page 2: clearance, 246 labelled structures. Each page compares structure-only models (REF0, top row) with the same backbone supplied with a four-channel one-hot pH 7.4 ionisation category (REF74, bottom row). Columns are GraphConv, Chemprop and frozen ChemBERTa with a trained MLP head. The cohort, grouped folds and nine training seeds are matched. Both arms use equal explicit endpoint loss weights and no mass-balance penalty.
Each point represents one structure. The horizontal coordinate is the mean of its nine out-of-fold log10 predictions; the vertical coordinate is its observed log10 endpoint. The average corresponds to a geometric mean prediction in physical units. R-squared is 1 - sum((observed - predicted)^2)/sum((observed - mean(observed))^2), evaluated on these displayed averages. It differs from the mean of nine separately scored seeds used in the main comparison. The dashed line denotes equality. Axes are shared across conditions and models for each endpoint, and every labelled point is retained.
Colours and marker shapes show the original pH 7.4 category in both rows; they annotate the baseline plots but were not supplied to the baseline models. Acid-only, base-only, neither-active and dual-active are operational acidic/basic pKa threshold categories. They do not identify resolved microspecies. This comparison measures the effect of supplying ionisation information and does not isolate a learned embedding mechanism. No new training was required.
