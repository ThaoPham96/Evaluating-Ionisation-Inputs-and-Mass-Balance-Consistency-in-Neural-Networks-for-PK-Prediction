CATEGORICAL pH FOLLOW-UP | INTERPRETATION OF FROZEN SCORES

The comparison pools five held-out folds within each of nine seeds for 327
structures in 238 frozen groups. MAE differences are first arm minus comparator.
Positive values mean higher error. F and PPB use percentage points, and the
other five endpoints use log10 units. Percent changes and interval bounds use
the same fixed comparator mean MAE. Crossed intervals resample whole seed
trajectories and frozen groups. Only the six shared-recipe F/Vd headline rows
use adjusted 99.17% intervals. All other intervals below are exploratory 95%.
Sample SD and favourable seed counts describe paired seed variation.

1. Width-matched primary comparison

No shared-recipe ALL4 versus REP4 result met the primary reduction rule.
ChemBERTa Vd MAE increased from 0.4654 to 0.4772, +0.01172 log10 units
(adjusted interval +0.00370 to +0.01989), or +2.52% (+0.80% to +4.27%).
The increase occurred in all nine seeds and all five outer folds. The other
five adjusted intervals span zero. In order GraphConv, Chemprop, ChemBERTa:

  Vd: +3.24% [-1.37, +8.28], +2.31% [-4.46, +8.67], +2.52% [+0.80, +4.27].
  F:  -0.47% [-4.87, +4.65], -1.68% [-8.63, +5.36], +0.62% [-0.93, +2.11].

F estimates correspond to -0.120, -0.454 and +0.146 percentage points on
172 labels from 135 groups. Lower F errors occurred in 7/9, 8/9 and 1/9
seeds respectively. The adjusted intervals still span reductions and increases.
Vd uses 259 labels from 196 groups. Vd MAE was higher in 9/9 GraphConv,
7/9 Chemprop and 9/9 ChemBERTa seeds. Independent tuning kept all three Vd

4. Independent tuning and metric shifts

Tuned Chemprop gives the main exploratory improvements over REP4. ALL4
lowers PPB MAE by 1.64 percentage points [-3.22, -0.28], or -8.14%
[-15.99, -1.40]. It lowers Cmax MAE by 0.0591 log10 units [-0.1185,
-0.0064], or -6.00% [-12.04, -0.65]. Each point estimate favours ALL4 in
all nine seeds and all five outer-fold averages. Shared-recipe PPB and
Cmax estimates have the same sign (-2.27% and -2.32%) but both intervals
span zero. GraphConv Cmax moves from -2.05% under the shared recipe to
+1.59% under tuning. ChemBERTa Cmax is about +0.70% in both. These results
support prioritising the tuned Chemprop PPB/Cmax findings as exploratory,
with no matching broad gain across recipes and backbones.

Shared-recipe REF74 gave lower Chemprop MAE point estimates than tuned ALL4
for both endpoints. PPB was 18.33726 versus 18.52103 percentage points,
an ALL4 change of +0.18378 percentage points (+1.00221%). Cmax was 0.924760
versus 0.925287 log10 units, an ALL4 change of +0.000527 (+0.05702%). The
tuned REP4 MAEs were 20.16228 percentage points and 0.984356 log10 units,
respectively. These cross-procedure point comparisons are descriptive.
