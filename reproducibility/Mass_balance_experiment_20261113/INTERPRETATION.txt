INTERPRETATION FOR INDEPENDENT SCIENTIFIC REVIEW

Main finding
Adding the selected positive consistency loss increased the fraction of predicted
triads within 1.25-fold in all three model families. Mean triad prediction error
changed very little, with all paired overall accuracy intervals including zero.

                      Consistency baseline -> loss   Relative triad MAE change
GraphConv                       40.59% -> 92.79%                  -0.306%
Chemprop                        41.30% -> 75.83%                  +0.145%
Frozen ChemBERTa + MLP           38.98% -> 85.48%                  -0.053%

Paired accuracy delta in log10 MAE [95% group-bootstrap interval]:
GraphConv     -0.001435 [-0.005574, +0.002481]
Chemprop      +0.000667 [-0.004260, +0.004744]
ChemBERTa     -0.000248 [-0.001637, +0.001083]
Consistency gains, percentage points [95% interval]:
GraphConv     +52.19 [47.92,56.09]
Chemprop      +34.52 [30.98,38.43]
ChemBERTa     +46.50 [42.75,49.92]

What this supports
The penalty is an effective way to change output consistency across these three
model families under the shared protocol. It does not demonstrate that this
training approach improves prediction accuracy overall. An interval crossing
zero does not prove equivalence or an absence of accuracy cost.

Post-hoc controls change the interpretation
Exact orthogonal correction produces 100% consistency by construction, with
relative triad MAE increases of 0.370%, 0.246% and 0.066% for GraphConv, Chemprop
and ChemBERTa respectively. Exact half-life-only correction increases triad MAE
by 0.422%, 1.185% and 0.736%. These are point estimates without the corresponding
paired exact-control intervals yet. High consistency is therefore not unique
to training with a loss and cannot by itself establish an advantage over a
simple deterministic correction.
Inner-selected partial correction is a separate comparison. Orthogonal alpha
was zero in all five GraphConv folds, three Chemprop folds and no ChemBERTa folds.
Selected orthogonal consistency was 40.59%,56.55%,69.88%; selected half-life-only
consistency was 51.34%,54.96%,65.79%. Their overall accuracy intervals include zero.
The positive loss is required to intervene, whereas alpha may select no change.
The zero-eligible lambda policy should be shown in supporting results so this
selection asymmetry remains visible.

Label compatibility limits the biological interpretation
Observed same-row triads are only 53.86% consistent within 1.25-fold, with median
disagreement 1.198-fold. Some disagreement can reflect differing source definitions
or measurement contexts. The 0.05959 log-MAE compatibility floor is a diagnostic
for exactly coherent predictions, not a model score or evidence that a finite
model must lose accuracy. The claim should concern algebraic consistency under
heterogeneous curated labels, not proven biological fidelity or superiority.

Further analyses before finalising a figure or Results text
Inspect fixed-lambda and fixed-alpha trade-offs on common three-seed summaries;
plot nine-seed selected-policy results with their paired intervals separately.
Examine endpoint shifts and prediction spread to identify which outputs absorb
the penalty. Evaluate the completed depth, encoder, task-weight and single-task
blocks and the prespecified source-definition sensitivity (six central-volume
records, cannabidiol regimen mismatch, ergoloid context). Also stratify by
observed compatibility descriptively. No new training is required for those
analyses. The additional metric suite and subgroup/curve intervals are pending.
Secondary endpoint contrasts are exploratory; the intervals are not multiplicity
adjusted. Do not select a narrative from isolated borderline endpoint effects.

Suggested narrative
First establish the compatibility of observed labels; then show the change in
consistency and prediction error when adding the loss across model families;
compare training-time intervention with partial/exact output correction; finally
use supplementary robustness results to explain the scope of the effect.
The experiment can stand alone without an architecture-ranking or novelty claim.
