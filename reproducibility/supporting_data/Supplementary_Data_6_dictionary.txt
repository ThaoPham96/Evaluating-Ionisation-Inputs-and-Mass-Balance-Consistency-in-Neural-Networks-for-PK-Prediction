Supplementary Data 6: structure inventory and additional saved-prediction summaries

No models were trained or selected for these analyses. Predictions retain the original
out-of-fold assignments and nine fitted seeds (101,202,303,404,505,606,707,808,909).
All intervals are nominal, paired whole-group percentile bootstrap intervals with
10,000 draws, conditional on the fitted models; no multiple-comparison adjustment.

full_structure_inventory.csv: all 952 original structure-level records. canonical_smiles
is the actual model input, not the stripped grouping parent. Raw scalar labels retain
their original units: t1/2(hour), VD(liter), Cl(liter/hour), Cmax_uM, Tmax (hours),
F(percentage) and PPB(percentage). Empty entries are missing labels. source_rows is a
JSON array of original row indices. group_id and outer_fold reproduce the grouped
evaluation assignments. same_row_complete_triad identifies the 674 eligible observed
triads. observed_triad_endpoint_count counts nonmissing half-life, volume and clearance.
The other identity/context fields are retained from the reconciled dataset; unknown
definitions remain unknown and are not inferred from the prediction residual.

input_composition.csv: ionisation-only minus structure-only accuracy on the numerical
327-structure cohort, partitioned into single_component_no_charged_atoms (244),
single_component_charged_atoms (6), and multiple_components (77). These groups classify
the recorded input graph; they are not pH-dependent physiological charge states.
Multiple components include hydrates and formulation components as well as counterions.
All original fitted models and training cohorts are retained in this subgroup analysis.

partial_triad_accuracy.csv: positive-penalty minus baseline errors in the full cohort,
separately for one observed triad label (187 structures), two labels (46), and their
pooled subgroup (233). Scoring includes only observed endpoint labels. Test-compound
observations are not provided to the prediction model at inference.

encoding_contrasts.csv: all six pairwise contrasts among C0 (structure only), C1 (charge
sign), C2 (one-hot) and C3 (learned embedding) on the 494-structure assigned-class cohort.
comparison is condition minus reference. All seven endpoints are retained; the paper's
Table S15 shows volume, with 388 labels in 258 groups.

For the preceding three contrast files, reference_mae and condition_mae average the
individual-seed MAEs. absolute_delta is condition minus reference. relative_pct is
100*absolute_delta/reference_mae. primary_low/high are relative-percent interval bounds;
absolute_low/high are bounds in the endpoint's scoring units. Denominators are
recomputed within each paired bootstrap draw. The logarithmic endpoints (t_half, Vd,
CL, Cmax, Tmax) use log10 units; F and PPB use percentage-point errors.
n counts eligible labels, n_groups eligible chemical groups, n_seeds fitted seeds.
bootstrap_draws is 10000. nonempty_draws counts resamples containing eligible labels.
Empty draws are excluded jointly from numerator and denominator. Intervals are omitted
when fewer than five eligible groups or fewer than 9500 nonempty draws contribute;
point estimates are retained. This reporting rule does not establish reliable coverage
in the other small subgroups: the clearance subsets with 13 or 15 labels remain
exploratory and sensitive to individual groups.

factorial_strata.csv: selected original Supplementary Data 1 rows, unchanged numerically.
F identifies the fixed-capacity shared-penalty factorial experiment. Observed strata
contain 152 complete triads at D<=1.5 and 90 at D>1.5. Conditions 00,10,01,11 mean
structure only, ionisation only, loss only, and both. 11-10_structure_scale is adding
the loss after ionisation, expressed as percentage points of structure-only triad MAE.
interaction = 100*(M11-M10-M01+M00)/M00. See the Data 1 dictionary for all inherited fields.

threshold_counts.csv and threshold_boundary_records.csv: exact comparisons against the
archived numerical pKa values, with acidic pKa<pH and basic pKa>pH. Equality leaves its
channel inactive. class_code=acid_active+2*base_active: 0 neither, 1 acid only, 2 base
only, 3 both. The boundary file lists only records exactly equal to a tested threshold.

stopping_epochs.csv: selected configuration ID, penalty weight and median best epoch
used for full952 baseline/positive fits and class494 C0-C3 fits, one record per outer
fold and condition. All nine final seeds use that epoch count. It is the median best
checkpoint epoch, not the inner-fit termination epoch after patience.

manifest.json records source checksums, interval conventions and CSV checksums.
The reproducible saved_prediction_checks.py script performs these analyses without fitting.
