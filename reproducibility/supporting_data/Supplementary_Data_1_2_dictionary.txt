SUPPLEMENTARY DATA 1 AND 2

Data 1 contains the complete accuracy and consistency contrasts. Data 2 contains
the algebraic post-processing comparisons, including direct and derived MAEs.
Every row is a model/condition comparison within one cohort and observed-label
stratum. No rows were selected by interval direction.

cohort: full952 = full952-structure cohort; unified327 = paired numerical-pKa
subset; class494 = cohort with assigned pH7.4 ionisation classes.
protocol: F = fixed-capacity main comparisons; F_pH = single-pH inputs;
F_supp3 = supplementary encoder/depth/task-weight/single-task comparisons with
three seeds; F_class_encoding = category encodings in494 structures;
F_zero_policy = zero-inclusive inner penalty selection sensitivity.
model: ChemBERTa_head denotes frozen ChemBERTa with a trained prediction head.

Main condition identifiers: 00 = structure only; 10 = ionisation category only;
01 = mass-balance penalty only; 11 = both. baseline = structure only;
selected_positive/loss = the selected positive penalty; zero_eligible = penalty
selection also allowing zero. In pH comparisons REF0 = structure only, REF74 =
pH7.4 category; PH2/PH5/PH65/PH8 supply categories at2/5/6.5/8.0. In the class
cohort C0 = structure only, C1 = charge-sign code, C2 = one-hot category,
C3 = learned categorical embedding. H:<condition>__derive_<endpoint>-original
compares one algebraically derived endpoint with the original prediction.

GraphConv_depth2_task_weight_sensitivity uses supervised weights
[1,5,5,1,1,1,1] in the training endpoint order
[t_half,Vd,CL,F,PPB,Cmax,Tmax]: five for volume and clearance, one otherwise.
Both zero-penalty and positive-penalty arms retain the outer fold's original
two-layer GraphConv configuration; the positive arm also inherits its selected
positive penalty weight. Inner calibration uses seed0, and each arm's final
epoch is the median of its five inner stopping epochs. Final seeds are
101,202,303. Configuration IDs for outer folds1..5 are10,2,10,2,10; inherited
positive lambdas are0.1,0.03,3,3,0.3. Median epochs for zero/positive arms are
72/70,93/112,44/44,102/106,93/82, respectively. These settings describe the
existing80-fit sensitivity (50 inner calibrations and30 final fits).

stratum: all uses every observed label for the endpoint; complete_triad uses
the same-row complete observed triads; observed_le_1.5 and observed_gt_1.5 split
complete triads by observed fold disagreement<=1.5 and>1.5. Internal consistency
in the all stratum uses all predicted triads, including incomplete observations.
n/n_groups identify the scored structures/groups; n_seeds/seeds identify fits.

metric: t_half,Vd,CL,Cmax,Tmax,F,PPB are endpoint MAEs. triad_mae gives equal
weight to the three log10 endpoint errors on complete observed triads.
seven_endpoint_average gives equal weight to seven endpoint relative changes.
mean_abs_residual is mean absolute log10 algebraic disagreement; within_1.25
is the percentage of predicted triads agreeing within1.25-fold.
unit is the absolute scoring unit: log10 for the firstfive endpoints and
percentage_points for bioavailability/protein binding.

denominator/denominator_condition identify the reference MAE used to scale a
relative result. denominator_mean is its full-cohort mean across fitted seeds.
absolute_delta, absolute_low/high: paired change and nominal95% whole-group
interval on the endpoint's absolute scoring scale. absolute_seed_sd describes
the SD of paired changes across fitted seeds separately.
relative_pct:100 times the mean paired MAE change divided by the declared
reference mean MAE. relative_seed_sd uses that same full-cohort denominator.
primary_low/high: nominal95% paired whole-group bootstrap percentiles; fitted
seeds are averaged, and the declared mean denominator is recomputed per draw.
fixed_group_low/high: groups resampled, denominator fixed at its full-cohort mean.
crossed_fixed_low/high: groups and whole fitted-seed trajectories resampled,
denominator fixed at its full-cohort mean.

scale=comparator_specific is a percentage change versus that comparator.
scale=common_structure_only is a percentage-point difference on the common
structure-only MAE scale, used for factorial interactions and contrasts bearing
the suffix structure_scale. Relative units/scales are not_applicable for
absolute-only consistency outcomes. Their absolute_delta/low/high remain valid.
terms gives the linear contrast coefficients. interaction uses11-10-01+00.

In prediction provenance, reference_fit_id identifies the same-input loss-off
fit for the specified protocol, input, model, outer fold and seed. This paired
fit identity is distinct from the statistical denominator: input comparisons
and interactions may use the protocol's structure-only loss-off comparator.
denominator_condition and scale specify the actual normalising MAE in each
contrast. Reused fixed-capacity reference aliases retain their archived fit IDs.

legacy_method/low/high identify the corresponding interval convention for
comparison. Direction fields describe whether each interval lies above/below
or spans zero; they are descriptive, unadjusted and exploratory. They are not
equivalence tests. Some requested H/stratum contrasts were newly calculated,
so legacy fields there denote the alternative convention rather than a
previously published result.

For Data2 accuracy rows, direct_prediction_mae is the original endpoint MAE
and derived_prediction_mae is the MAE after replacing the named prediction
by its algebraic derivation. Other outputs are unchanged. All three directions
were evaluated. Exact consistency is guaranteed by the construction.

Bootstrap resampling conditions on the fitted models, observed training data,
fixed folds and selected settings. It does not refit models or repeat tuning.
All intervals use10000 resamples. Whole-group draws are shared within cohorts.

FINAL F/T MATRIX: F_new is fixed-capacity R0/R2; T_new is input-tuned. P0 structure, P1 category, P2 raw pKa, P3 fractions. *_new_zero_policy uses inner-selected zero-inclusive choices. New export reference_fit_id identifies the same-input loss-off fit for protocol/input/model/fold/seed; it is NOT necessarily the comparator used for input effects or interactions. denominator_condition and scale specify each statistical contrast. Supplementary Data 3 T/R0 lists resolved configuration fields; R2 lists shared/separate/zero-inclusive lambdas and median epochs.
