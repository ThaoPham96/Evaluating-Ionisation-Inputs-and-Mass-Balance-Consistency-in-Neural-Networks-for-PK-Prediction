S2: Adding training loss or post-hoc correction to ionisation-only predictions. In A, each accuracy contrast and both interval bounds are divided by the fixed ionisation-only seed mean. B shows absolute mean log10 disagreement, with each model’s ionisation-only mean as a blue dashed reference line. Selected correction strengths use only inner validation predictions. Charcoal intervals are dashed for orthogonal correction and dotted for half-life-only correction. Open diamonds show exact controls (α = 1), which produce algebraic consistency by construction.

All intervals are equal-tailed percentile intervals from 10,000 paired resamples of whole scaffold/parent groups and whole seed trajectories. They condition on the observed training data, saved folds and selected recipes, and do not retrain models within each draw. Each seed pools its five held-out outer folds.

S3. Accuracy and internal consistency across four conditions

A. The triad macro MAE averages absolute log10 errors equally across half-life, volume of distribution and clearance on 242 observed same-row triads. Each dot pools the five held-out outer folds of one seed. Lines connect the same seed across conditions. Diamonds show the mean of nine seeds with 95% crossed bootstrap intervals. Structure only (00) has neither ionisation input nor a mass-balance penalty. Ionisation (10) adds the pH 7.4 category. Loss (01) adds the mass-balance penalty. Ionisation + loss (11), labelled Both on the axis, uses the ionisation category and the same fold-specific positive penalty as 01.

B. Fold disagreement is 10 raised to the absolute log10 residual, or the larger divided by the smaller of predicted half-life and the half-life implied by ln(2) times predicted volume divided by predicted clearance. A value of one indicates exact agreement. Each curve averages empirical CDFs computed separately for nine fitted seeds on all 327 held-out structures per seed. Higher curves indicate more consistent predictions. The panel shows 1–5-fold. At most 18 of 2,943 predictions per curve (0.61%) lie above 5-fold. The full CDF support is included in S3_plot_data.csv.


S4. Paired effects of adding the mass-balance loss to ionisation-informed models.

A. “Seven endpoints” shows the equal-weight average of seven endpoint percentage MAE changes. Each endpoint uses all observed labels: half-life 316, Vd 259, CL 246, Cmax 190, Tmax 249, F 172 and PPB 292, retaining zero F/PPB labels. Five endpoints use log10 MAE and F/PPB use percentage-point MAE. Each endpoint's paired MAE difference (both minus ionisation only) is divided by its fixed ionisation-only nine-seed mean MAE, multiplied by 100, and the seven changes are averaged within each seed. “Triad” shows the prespecified percentage change in raw triad macro log10 MAE on the 242 complete same-row triads, divided by the fixed ionisation-only triad mean. This triad macro metric averages raw log10 endpoint MAEs before normalisation; it therefore retains the original primary weighting, whereas Supplementary Figure S5 averages normalised endpoint MAEs.

B. Percentage change in mean absolute log10 triad residual on all 327 structures when adding the loss to ionisation-informed models. Estimates and interval bounds use the fixed ionisation-only mean across seeds as the denominator.

Dots show nine paired seed changes; diamonds show means. Thick lines show exploratory 95% percentile intervals. Thin lines show 99.1667% Bonferroni-adjusted intervals for the six prespecified primary comparisons (triad accuracy and disagreement across three models). The seven-endpoint summary is exploratory and has 95% intervals only. The 10,000 crossed bootstrap draws jointly resample whole structure groups and whole fitted seed trajectories across endpoints, preserving endpoint dependence and using each endpoint's resampled observed-label count. Normalising denominators remain fixed. Training data, folds, recipes and selection procedure remain fixed.

S5. Accuracy and internal agreement on the matched 242-structure complete-triad subset. Each endpoint MAE (half-life, Vd and CL) is divided by its fixed nine-seed structure-only mean, and the three ratios are averaged equally, centred at one and multiplied by 100. The vertical axis uses predicted triad residuals on the same 242 structures. Circles pair outcomes for nine fitted seeds and diamonds show means. Hollow marks show ionisation + loss. This relative endpoint average differs from the prespecified raw triad macro MAE in S3 and S4.
