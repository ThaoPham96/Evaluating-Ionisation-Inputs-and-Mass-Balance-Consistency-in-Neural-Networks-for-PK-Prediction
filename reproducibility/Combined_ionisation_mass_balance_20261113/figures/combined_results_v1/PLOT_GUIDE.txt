COMBINED RESULTS: PLOT GUIDE

Start with combined_overview: four conditions (structure only, pH 7.4
ionisation input, mass-balance loss, both) on the same cohort/folds/seeds.
Top row: triad prediction error, lower is better.
Bottom row: cumulative predicted triad agreement, higher curves are better.

adding_loss_tradeoff isolates Both minus Ionisation. The left panel is
prediction error; the right panel is internal disagreement. Seed dots and
bootstrap intervals are distinct sources of variability. Multiplicity-adjusted
intervals are the thin bars; exploratory 95% intervals are the thick bars.

combined_results_all_plots.pdf contains five pages:
1. Four-condition overview
2. Adding-loss trade-off
3. All seven endpoint errors (S1)
4. Four-cell accuracy interactions (S2)
5. Training loss versus post-hoc correction controls (S3)

Each main and supplementary figure also has a PNG, PDF and editable SVG.
Captions and plotted source tables sit beside their render.py scripts.
No models were retrained and no analysis tables were changed to make plots.
